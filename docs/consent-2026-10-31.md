# Consent notice version 2026-10-31

New registrations and contract submissions must explicitly accept the current
terms and privacy notice. The server records `consentVersion` and
`consentAcceptedAt` on the resulting user or contract. It rejects missing,
negative, or stale acknowledgments before creating the account or uploading
the contract to Cloudinary. Timestamps are generated by the server.

The acknowledgment covers account creation or authorized document submission,
storage, document processing, and attorney review. The displayed disclosure
explains that original documents and review records are retained, automatic
identifier masking may miss sensitive information, and screening requires
attorney review. Current document processing uses backend PDF parsing/Tesseract
and statutory retrieval with configured rules; external LLM/cloud OCR providers
are not active. No public AI model training is performed by the application.

For attorney uploads, the acknowledgment is that of the uploading attorney. It
does not prove consent from every person mentioned in the document. Existing
records without consent metadata remain unchanged and must not be described as
having accepted this notice retroactively.

The full displayed terms and privacy notice are in
`client/src/components/TermsModal.tsx` and `PrivacyPolicyModal.tsx`. Changes to
their material processing disclosures require a new version in both
`client/src/utils/consent.ts` and `server/src/utils/consent.js`, with a new notice
record. These technical records do not establish legal compliance by themselves.

## Compatibility and rollback

The two optional fields are additive; there is no migration or historical
backfill. Deploy frontend and backend together because new account creation
and uploads require the versioned acknowledgment. Rollback restores the prior
validation and UI together; retained consent metadata may remain in MongoDB.
